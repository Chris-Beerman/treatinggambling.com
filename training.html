Training for clinical teams

Practical trainings for treatment programs, group practices and agencies that want to screen for gambling and treat it well.

Plan a training

Topics

Pick one, or I’ll build a session around what your team is seeing.

  • Screening and assessment

    Brief screens, the PGSI, the DSM-5 criteria and how to ask about gambling so people answer honestly.

  • Sports betting in the legal era

    Apps, parlays, promos and live betting, and why they change how fast harm builds.

  • Trading, crypto and prediction markets

    When investing turns into gambling, and how to talk about it with clients who don’t call it gambling.

  • Money and safety first

    Protecting finances in the first weeks: access limits, self-exclusion, blocking tools and handing over control.

  • Suicide risk

    Why risk runs high with gambling disorder, and how to assess it when debt and shame are part of the picture.

  • Partners and families

    Helping the people around the gambler, including CRAFT, and what the evidence does and doesn’t show.

  • Gambling with alcohol and drugs

    Treating gambling inside a substance use program, and what has to change.

  • Relapse and long-term recovery

    Triggers tied to the sports calendar, lapses, and building a life that doesn’t need the action.

Formats

  • Focused session. 60 to 90 minutes on one topic. Good for a staff meeting or lunch.
  • Half-day workshop. Screening through treatment planning, with case practice.
  • Full-day workshop. The whole arc, from first screen to relapse prevention.
  • Ongoing team consultation. A regular hour where your clinicians bring their gambling cases.

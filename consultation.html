What we can work on

Bring a de-identified case and the questions you have about it. Common ones:

  • Is this gambling disorder, and how severe is it?
  • Is trading, crypto or a prediction market the real problem here?
  • What should we do first about access to money and betting apps?
  • How do I involve a spouse or parent without making things worse?
  • How do I assess suicide risk with someone in heavy debt?
  • Is outpatient enough, or do they need a higher level of care?
  • They relapsed. What now?

Gambling often comes with alcohol or other substance use. We can work on both.

Who it’s for

Therapists, counselors, social workers, psychologists, psychiatric providers and clinical supervisors. You don’t need to be a gambling specialist to book.

Workplaces and EAPs

Sports betting happens on work phones, during work hours, with money people can’t afford to lose. I help organizations notice it and respond well.

Talk about your workplace

  • Manager and HR training

    What betting harm looks like at work, what to say when someone discloses it, and where to refer.

  • EAP counselor training

    Screening and short-term work for EAP clinicians, plus when to refer to specialized treatment.

  • Employee education

    A plain-language session for staff on how betting apps work and where to get help.

  • Policy consultation

    Help writing a response plan that treats gambling like the health problem it is.

What this isn’t

I don’t evaluate or treat your employees through this work, and I don’t report on individuals. Employees who want therapy can reach my practice or any provider directly and privately.
